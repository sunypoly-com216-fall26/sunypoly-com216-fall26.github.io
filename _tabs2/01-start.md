## Should you take this course?

> Learn to use generative AI intelligently, responsibly, and with your own judgment intact — while studying how communication technologies have shaped human thought across ten thousand years of history.

Here is the invitation: spend your first semester learning to use generative AI on purpose. Not secretly, not fearfully, and not as a magic answer machine. Use it directly. Test it. Push it. Notice what it does well, where it gets lazy, where it sounds more confident than it should, and where it genuinely helps you do work you could not have done alone.

This course is designed for first-year students who are curious and a little adventurous. You do not need prior experience with generative AI, and this is not a coding course. You do need to be willing to use it often, compare tools, save your conversations, and keep your own judgment in charge.

If you are thinking, "AI is interesting and I want to learn how to use it well" — you are probably in the right place. If you would rather avoid generative AI altogether, this is probably not the right first-year experience for you.

### What you will learn to do

- Use generative AI to ask better questions, read difficult material, draft, revise, plan, and study.
- Tell the difference between a useful response, a polished but weak response, and a response that is simply wrong.
- Keep a record of how your thinking develops when you work with a model.
- Use these tools in ways that are appropriate for college and responsible to other people.
- Understand why this technology matters in the longer history of human communication — and what makes it genuinely different from everything that came before it.

### Questions this course starts from

- How can generative AI help me learn without doing the learning for me?
- What changes when machines can generate language back at us?
- How do I know when a model is useful, wrong, shallow, or overconfident?
- What does it mean to write, read, and think in college now?
- How do I use generative AI without letting it replace my own judgment?

### Technology expectations

Students in this course are provided access to paid generative AI tools. You do not need to buy a subscription on your own. You will, however, need to be willing to use commercial generative AI systems regularly as part of the coursework.

That means we also practice basic rules for privacy and academic honesty. Do not put sensitive personal, medical, financial, or family information into these systems. If a tool creates an accessibility problem, we will adjust the workflow. The course assumes frequent use of generative AI — but visible, documented, and accountable use.
